Four types of analytics:

1. Descriptive Analytics
Shows what happened through dashboards, reports, KPIs, and trend tracking.

2. Diagnostic Analytics
Explains why it happened using root cause analysis, segmentation, and comparisons.

3. Predictive Analytics
Forecasts what may happen next using patterns, models, and historical behavior.

4. Prescriptive Analytics
Recommends what to do next using optimization, simulations, and decision logic.
